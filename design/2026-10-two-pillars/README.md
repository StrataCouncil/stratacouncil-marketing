# Two pillars: Council Training and Stratasphere (October 2026, on hold)

Mock-ups for giving Stratasphere more weight on the marketing site while
keeping Council Training first. Not built; kept for later. They are static
pages, not part of the site, and aren't served.

- `single-page/`: one homepage with both pillars. Hero with two cards, two
  training sections, four Stratasphere sections (where council time goes;
  prepare and run; remember; security), "Bring it to your council" with the
  draft trial motion, pricing, FAQ.
- `three-pages/`: a homepage split into two columns, each leading to its own
  page (`/training`, `/stratasphere`). Every button on those pages goes to
  sign-up, with `?start=training` or `?start=stratasphere` so the app could
  open the matching tab after the first sign-in (not built).

Decisions made along the way:

- Every button leads to the sign-up form; no demo requests.
- Pricing lives in the FAQ (the price chart in "How much does Stratasphere
  cost?"), not in its own section.
- Security wording: our hosting provider is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliant
  (we aren't); data stored in Canada; bank-grade encryption; row-level
  security; personal information stripped before anything reaches
  Stratasphere's AI; two-factor sign-in available to every account. BC's
  PIPA only.
- The precedent pool isn't advertised: it stays in the Terms of Service and
  the council slide deck.
